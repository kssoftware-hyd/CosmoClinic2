Frequently asked questions

Who is a good candidate for a hair transplant?

It depends on your hair loss pattern, donor hair availability, scalp health and general health. The doctor will assess you in person and tell you honestly whether a transplant is suitable.

Is the procedure painful?

Hair transplants are usually done under local anaesthesia, so the area is numbed. Some mild discomfort afterwards is normal and your doctor will advise on aftercare.

How long does the procedure take?

Duration depends on the number of grafts planned. The doctor will give you an estimate after your assessment.

What is the recovery time?

Most people return to routine activities within a few days, but healing and hair growth take months. You will receive written aftercare instructions.

What does a hair transplant cost?

Cost depends on the number of grafts and the technique chosen. The clinic will share a clear quote after your consultation.

What should I check before choosing a hair clinic?

Look for doctor-led assessment, clear information on risks and limits, a hygienic setup, real patient results shared with consent, and no pressure to decide quickly.

How do I book a consultation?

Use the enquiry form, call us, or message us on WhatsApp. The clinic will confirm your appointment with you directly.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Results differ between individuals and no clinic can guarantee an outcome.
